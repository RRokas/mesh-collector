Route = routers the latest reading passed through: the one that heard it over Bluetooth first, the one that delivered it last. BLE hops come from the tag payload; router hops = route length − 1; total = both. "?" marks a reading from an older router version that didn't report its path.

"Heard first" counts readings this router was the origin of. "Dup copies" are records a router delivered that another path had already delivered — normal under at-least-once delivery. Delivery delay = our receive time minus the router's sent_at for its last POST; a large value means a slow uplink or a wrong router clock.
